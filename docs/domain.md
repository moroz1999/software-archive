## Project Domain
The project is a catalog archive of software and the people, groups and events around it.
Read detailed files only WHEN you get specific domain questions.

## Common rules
- Every title in the archive — of software, a group, a party, an alternative title — is required, cannot be blank and is at most 255 characters long.

## Domains

### software
A piece of software: the work itself, with its titles, languages, links and credits.
Detailed logic and structure: [domain/software.md](domain/software.md)

### authors
Real people, groups and the labels works are released under.
Detailed logic and structure: [domain/authors.md](domain/authors.md)

### events
Demoparties and competitions, their compos and the placements of entries.
Detailed logic and structure: [domain/events.md](domain/events.md)

### hardware
Platforms, the hardware software runs on, and the platform switch over the whole archive.
Detailed logic and structure: [domain/hardware.md](domain/hardware.md)

### geo
Countries and cities, and where authors and events are located.
Detailed logic and structure: [domain/geo.md](domain/geo.md)
