## Project Domain
The project is a catalog archive of software and the people, groups and events around it.
Read detailed files only WHEN you get specific domain questions.

## Common rules
- Every title in the archive — of software, a group, a party, an alternative title — is required, cannot be blank and is at most 255 characters long.

## Domains

### software
A piece of software: the work itself, with its titles, languages, links and credits.
Detailed logic and structure: [domain/software.md](domain/software.md)

### categories
The one tree of categories software is sorted by, for every platform.
Detailed logic and structure: [domain/categories.md](domain/categories.md)

### authors
Real people, groups and the labels works are released under.
Detailed logic and structure: [domain/authors.md](domain/authors.md)

### events
Demoparties and competitions, their compos and the placements of entries.
Detailed logic and structure: [domain/events.md](domain/events.md)

### hardware
Platforms, the hardware software runs on, and the platform switch over the whole archive.
Detailed logic and structure: [domain/hardware.md](domain/hardware.md)

### geo
Countries and cities, and where authors and events are located.
Detailed logic and structure: [domain/geo.md](domain/geo.md)

### localization
The interface languages visitors can use and which parts of the archive follow that choice.
Detailed logic and structure: [domain/localization.md](domain/localization.md)

### search
Quick and full search over software, people and groups, and the filters of the software catalog.
Detailed logic and structure: [domain/search.md](domain/search.md)

### voting
How signed-in users rate software, and how the rating of a work is made of the votes.
Detailed logic and structure: [domain/voting.md](domain/voting.md)

### users
Accounts, how visitors register, confirm and sign in, and what each user group may do.
Detailed logic and structure: [domain/users.md](domain/users.md)

### imports
One import for every source archive: how software, authors and places are matched, what is added and that nothing of the archive is rewritten.
Detailed logic and structure: [domain/imports.md](domain/imports.md)

### ai-assistant
The chat assistant of trusted editors: it researches the archive and proposes plans of changes that the editor approves.
Detailed logic and structure: [domain/ai-assistant.md](domain/ai-assistant.md)
