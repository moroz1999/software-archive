# PageLayout

`PageLayout` — the frame of a routed page: the page's only level 1 heading over its content blocks, centred within a maximal width. Independent content blocks are its direct children and get the large block gap between them. `title` is usually text; a page whose heading comes with its data passes a skeleton bone while it loads. `actions` — the page's buttons, on the title's line at its end (below it on narrow screens). `menu` — what can be done with what the page shows, a [PopoverMenu](popover-menu.md) right after the text of the title. `breadcrumbs` — the way down to the page ([Breadcrumbs](breadcrumbs.md)), over the title, with a larger gap under them; a page with them starts closer to the bar of the site (`--page-layout-breadcrumbs-padding-top`), a page without them shows none. The page also names the browser tab: `tabTitle`, else the title when it is text, then the name of the site — "Software — Ретротека"; while the title is a bone, the name of the site alone. The name of the site is one constant of the app.
