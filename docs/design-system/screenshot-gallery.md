# ScreenshotGallery

- `ScreenshotGallery` — screenshots as a grid of 4:3 thumbnails, each a button named "Screenshot n of N". A thumbnail opens its picture whole in a wide `Dialog` titled by `title`, which goes round through the pictures by its Previous and Next buttons and the arrow keys and closes by Escape, by its cross or by the backdrop.
- `ScreenshotGallerySkeleton` — the place of a gallery while its screenshots load: thumbnail bones in the same grid.
