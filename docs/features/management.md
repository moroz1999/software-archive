# Management

The administrator-only part of the archive groups category and user maintenance and the imports under one section of the main menu.

Its pages share tab navigation: Categories, Users and Imports. A visitor or a signed-in non-administrator opening a management address sees the not found page.

Category maintenance lives under `/management/categories`, including its adding and editing pages ([category-catalog.md](category-catalog.md), [category-editing.md](category-editing.md)).

User maintenance lives under `/management/users` ([user-management.md](user-management.md)).

Imports from other archives are started under `/management/imports` ([vektor-import.md](vektor-import.md)).
