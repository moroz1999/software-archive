# Management

The administrator-only part of the archive groups category and user maintenance under one section of the main menu.

Its pages share tab navigation: Categories and Users. A visitor or a signed-in non-administrator opening a management address sees the not found page.

Category maintenance lives under `/management/categories`, including its adding and editing pages ([category-catalog.md](category-catalog.md), [category-editing.md](category-editing.md)).

User maintenance lives under `/management/users` ([user-management.md](user-management.md)).
