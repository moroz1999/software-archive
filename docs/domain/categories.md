# Categories

Categories sort software by kind: games and their genres, demoscene productions, system software, applications, the press and so on.

- The categories form one tree, the same for every platform. A category at the top of the tree has no parent; any other has exactly one.
- The tree came over from zxart.ee as it was there, in English: its categories, their nesting and the order of the subcategories of each category.
- A category has a title. It follows the common title rules (see the domain index). Two categories may share a title under different parents ("Misc", "Games").
- The subcategories of a category keep their order. A category added or moved under another parent goes last there.
- Categories are added, edited and deleted in the archive; for now anyone can do it. Editing changes the title and the parent: a category can go under any other category or to the top, but never under itself or one of its own subcategories.
- Deleting a category deletes all its subcategories with it. Software in them stays in the archive and only leaves these categories.
- Software can be in any number of categories, none included, at any depth of the tree. Being in a category does not put software in its parent or its subcategories: each category it is in is chosen on its own.
- Software is never in a category together with one above it: being in a category already says it is of every category above. Of a branch only the deepest category chosen stays; choosing a category takes the software out of every category above it. This holds however the categories came together: chosen at once, or brought onto one branch by moving a category — the move itself takes the software out of every category it put above another of the software's own. Software stored before the rule was taken out of the categories above its others.
- Software is put only in categories of the archive; a category deleted while software is being put in it refuses that software's saving.
- Software shows each of its categories with its whole branch, from the top of the tree down to it, in the order of the tree.
- Deleting software takes it out of its categories.
