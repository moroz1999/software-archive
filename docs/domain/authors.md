# Authors

The archive separates who someone is from the names they use.

## Person
A real person. The real name is optional; a person may be known only through aliases. Can have notes.

## Group
A team that releases works. Has a name, notes and one or more group types.

## Alias
A name a person or a group is publicly known by. An owner can have several aliases; one of them can be marked as the display name.
Aliases are members of groups and can be tied to cities.
Works are credited to aliases, not to people — see [software.md](software.md).

## Main name
A person is shown under one main name: the alias marked as the display name; without one, the first of their aliases in alphabetical order; without aliases, the real name. A person without any name is not an author of the archive.

## Author catalog
The catalog is where visitors find authors. It lists people and their aliases mixed together, sorted by name, 100 entries per page:
- a person appears under their main name;
- each other alias of a person appears as an entry of its own, with the main name it belongs to; it leads to the same person;
- aliases of groups are not authors and are not listed.

Visitors narrow the catalog:
- by the first letter of the entry's name: a Latin letter A–Z, a Cyrillic letter А–Я, or `#` for names starting with anything else (digits, symbols, other scripts). Case does not matter; Ё counts as Е;
- by country and by city, where the person is located (any of their locations). Cities to choose from are those of the chosen country.

## Author page
The page of a person: the main name, the real name, notes, all their aliases, their locations (the primary one first) and the groups their aliases are members of.
