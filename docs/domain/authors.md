# Authors

The archive separates who someone is from the names they use.

## Person
A real person. The real name is optional; a person may be known only through aliases. Can have notes.

## Group
A team that releases works. Has a name, notes and one or more group types.

## Alias
A name a person or a group is publicly known by. An owner can have several aliases; one of them can be marked as the display name.
Aliases are members of groups and can be tied to cities.
Works are credited to aliases, not to people — see [software.md](software.md).
